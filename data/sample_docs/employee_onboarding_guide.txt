EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING GUIDE
Version 2.0 | Human Resources Department
Last Updated: March 2025

WELCOME TO THE ORGANIZATION

Welcome to our team! This guide will help you navigate your first weeks and ensure
a smooth transition into your new role. Please read through this document carefully
and reach out to your manager or HR representative with any questions.

1. FIRST DAY CHECKLIST

1.1 Before Arrival
- Complete all pre-boarding paperwork in the HR portal
- Set up your corporate email account using the credentials provided
- Download required applications (Teams, Outlook, VPN client)
- Review the organization chart for your department

1.2 Day One Activities
- Check in with Reception at Main Lobby (Building A)
- Collect your employee badge and parking pass
- Meet your onboarding buddy (assigned by HR)
- Complete IT equipment setup with the Help Desk
- Attend New Employee Orientation (10:00 AM - 12:00 PM)
- Lunch with your team (arranged by your manager)
- Complete mandatory security awareness training

1.3 First Week Goals
- Complete all required compliance training modules
- Set up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for all systems
- Schedule 1:1 meetings with key stakeholders
- Review team documentation and project backlogs
- Complete benefits enrollment (deadline: 30 days from start)

2. SYSTEMS AND TOOLS

2.1 Core Applications
- Email and Calendar: Microsoft Outlook / Exchange Online
- Communication: Microsoft Teams
- Document Management: SharePoint and OneDrive
- Project Management: Jira / Azure DevOps
- Knowledge Base: Confluence
- HR Portal: Workday

2.2 Access Requests
To request access to additional systems:
1. Submit a request through the IT Service Portal
2. Your manager must approve the request
3. IT will provision access within 24 business hours
4. You will receive confirmation via email

2.3 VPN and Remote Work
- Install the corporate VPN client from the IT portal
- VPN is required for accessing internal resources remotely
- Remote work policy allows up to 3 days per week
- Equipment for home office can be requested through IT

3. COMPANY POLICIES

3.1 Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to:
- Act with integrity and professionalism
- Respect diversity and inclusion
- Maintain confidentiality of company information
- Report ethical concerns through the Ethics Hotline
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations

3.2 Working Hours
- Standard hours: 9:00 AM - 5:30 PM local time
- Flexible start time between 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM (with manager approval)
- Core collaboration hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM
- Overtime requires prior manager approval
- Time tracking through the HR portal

3.3 Leave Policy
- Annual Leave: 20 days per year (prorated for partial years)
- Sick Leave: 10 days per year
- Personal Days: 3 days per year
- Parental Leave: 16 weeks paid (primary), 8 weeks paid (secondary)
- Bereavement Leave: 5 days for immediate family
- Leave requests submitted through Workday at least 2 weeks in advance

3.4 Professional Development
- Annual learning budget: $2,500 per employee
- Conference attendance: up to 2 conferences per year
- Internal training courses available through Learning Portal
- Tuition reimbursement for approved degree programs (up to $10,000/year)
- Mentorship program available (apply through HR portal)

4. BENEFITS OVERVIEW

4.1 Health and Wellness
- Medical insurance (3 plan options, effective Day 1)
- Dental and vision coverage
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- Gym membership subsidy ($50/month)
- Annual health screening

4.2 Financial Benefits
- 401(k) with 6% company match (immediate vesting)
- Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP) - 15% discount
- Life insurance (2x annual salary, company-paid)
- Short and long-term disability insurance
- Commuter benefits (pre-tax)

4.3 Additional Perks
- Flexible spending accounts (FSA/HSA)
- Employee referral bonus: $5,000 - $10,000
- Annual performance bonus (target: 10-20% of salary)
- Service awards at 5, 10, 15, 20 year milestones
- Employee discount program

5. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

5.1 Goal Setting
- Set SMART goals within first 30 days with your manager
- Goals aligned with team and organizational objectives
- Mid-year goal review and adjustment
- Year-end performance evaluation

5.2 Review Cycle
- 30-Day Check-in: Initial adjustment and feedback
- 60-Day Check-in: Progress on onboarding goals
- 90-Day Review: Formal probation review
- Semi-Annual Review: Mid-year performance discussion
- Annual Review: Comprehensive performance evaluation

5.3 Feedback Culture
- Continuous feedback encouraged through Teams and 1:1s
- 360-degree feedback for management roles
- Anonymous feedback surveys conducted quarterly
- Recognition program: Peer-to-peer kudos in Teams
