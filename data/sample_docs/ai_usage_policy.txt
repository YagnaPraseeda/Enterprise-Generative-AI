ENTERPRISE AI USAGE POLICY
Version 2.1 | Effective Date: January 15, 2025
Classification: Internal Use Only

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy establishes the governance framework for the use of Artificial Intelligence
(AI) and Machine Learning (ML) technologies across all business units. It applies to
all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who develop, deploy, or interact
with AI systems within the organization.

2. APPROVED AI USE CASES

2.1 Customer Service Automation
AI-powered chatbots and virtual assistants may be deployed for customer-facing
interactions, provided they meet the following criteria:
- All responses must be grounded in approved knowledge bases
- Escalation to human agents must be available within 2 interactions
- Customer data handling must comply with GDPR and CCPA requirements
- Response accuracy must maintain a minimum 95% threshold

2.2 Document Processing and Analysis
AI systems may be used for automated document processing including:
- Invoice processing and validation
- Contract analysis and key term extraction
- Compliance document review
- Internal knowledge base management

2.3 Data Analytics and Forecasting
Approved use cases include:
- Sales forecasting and demand planning
- Customer behavior analysis
- Operational efficiency optimization
- Risk assessment and fraud detection

3. PROHIBITED USES

The following AI applications are strictly prohibited:
- Autonomous decision-making in hiring or termination processes
- Surveillance of employee activities without explicit consent
- Generation of synthetic media (deepfakes) for any purpose
- Automated credit scoring without human oversight
- Any use that violates applicable laws or regulations

4. DATA GOVERNANCE FOR AI

4.1 Training Data Requirements
- All training data must be reviewed for bias before model development
- Personal identifiable information (PII) must be anonymized or pseudonymized
- Data provenance must be documented and traceable
- Synthetic data may be used only with approval from the Data Governance Board

4.2 Data Retention
- Model training data must be retained for a minimum of 3 years
- Inference logs must be retained for 1 year
- Model artifacts and weights must be versioned and archived

5. MODEL LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT

5.1 Development
- All models must undergo peer review before deployment
- Code must be stored in version-controlled repositories
- Experiment tracking must use approved MLOps platforms (MLflow)

5.2 Deployment
- Models must pass security review before production deployment
- A/B testing is required for customer-facing models
- Rollback procedures must be documented and tested

5.3 Monitoring
- Model performance must be monitored continuously
- Data drift detection must be implemented
- Alerts must be configured for performance degradation
- Quarterly model reviews are mandatory

6. RESPONSIBLE AI PRINCIPLES

6.1 Fairness
All AI systems must be evaluated for bias across protected characteristics
including race, gender, age, disability, and national origin.

6.2 Transparency
- Model decisions must be explainable to affected stakeholders
- AI-generated content must be clearly labeled
- Decision rationale must be logged and auditable

6.3 Accountability
- Each AI system must have a designated owner
- Incident response procedures must be established
- Regular audits must be conducted by the AI Ethics Board

7. COMPLIANCE AND ENFORCEMENT

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Immediate suspension of AI system access
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination
- Legal liability for applicable regulatory violations
